Granular

A local note editor built around precise, low-friction, multicolor text highlighting.

Approved scope

The distinctive idea

Highlight or remove text one character at a time—including spaces—rather than relying only on conventional selection. A movable bar and character-target pins mark a range; clicking the active color button applies that color to the entire span between the pins. Color-filled pointer bars in the sidebar make switching colors immediate.

Person → editor loop → proof

The pin endpoints activate with blue borders. Clicking the active color button applies it to the full range between them, including endpoints and spaces. For example, in “Obi is a good boy.”, the left pin targets “a” and the right pin targets the final “d” in “good”; the applied range is “a good”. Increment/decrement controls adjust it by character.

Who it's for

The learner is the first person to try Granular. Students are the first broader audience, followed by teachers; a student might emphasize copied reading or their own notes.

Why it matters

Make highlighting more convenient and expressive for learning and reference, while practicing reasonable scoping and finishing a working app.

The editor has a short note, a small set of distinct colors with add/activate pointer bars, a movable drag bar and character-target pins, and a separate color button beside the bar that applies the active color to the entire range bracketed by the pins. Activated pin endpoints are visibly marked; the applied range includes both endpoints and any spaces between them. Incremental controls provide character-by-character extension and removal, including across lines, with visible progressive movement and local persistence. Selecting a filled sidebar box switches the active color.
